Kent has a rich history of ballads.

Digital Drama working with heritage organisations in Sandwich and Deal ran training workshops for local residents to uncover, preserve and share memories of living in these vibrant East Kent communities. This project enabled participants to develop reminiscence, story-telling and interpretation skills to foster confidence and enhance employability, whilst preserving heritage. Local volunteers co-facilitated reminiscence sessions, gained skills in oral history recording and creative writing, to capture, interpret and preserve memories of older residents. Together they created a community ballad and a short film, featuring the newly recorded oral histories and archival material from museum collections.
